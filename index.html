Printing with the NIIMBOT app
  1. Open the PDF in the NIIMBOT app. Choose the 50 × 30 mm label.
  2. Print at 100% size. Do not use "fit" or "scale" — it makes the barcode bars uneven.
  3. Print one sticker first. Scan it with Stock check on the POS phone.
  4. It shows the right shoe? Print the rest.
  5. Bars look grey or broken? Make the print darker by one step and try again.
